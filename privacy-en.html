Shannian Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Shannian respects and protects user privacy. No account is required. The developer does not collect, store, sell or use personal data for tracking through this app.

Local data

Recitation records, practice goals, reading progress, personal notes and settings are stored locally on the user's device. They are not sent to the developer unless the user actively shares or exports a backup through the operating system.

Location permission

Shannian requests location permission only when the user actively searches for nearby vegetarian restaurants or temple dining halls. Location is used on the device for that search and is not collected or retained by the developer. Permission can be disabled in system settings.

Network and third-party services

Shannian retrieves public scripture, event and audio catalogues when needed. Nearby searches run on the device; iOS uses Apple Maps, while Android does not load online map tiles. Google Maps and YouTube links open in the corresponding app or browser, where the third party's privacy policy applies.

Buddhist dictionary lookups

When the user selects a scripture term and actively chooses Buddhist Dictionary, that selected term is sent to Deerpark (deerpark.app) to retrieve an explanation. No other content is sent, and nothing is sent without an active lookup.

Children's privacy

Shannian does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Updates and contact

This page will be updated if privacy practices change. Questions may be sent to pzdn2009@gmail.com.
