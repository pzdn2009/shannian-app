Śūraṅgama Mantra Memorization Guide

A 35-page web companion based on the 2022 Singapore memorization handbook

The guide preserves the original Chinese mantra text and handbook pagination. It includes the invocation, all five assemblies and dedication, with page masking, sentence-by-sentence reveal and local progress tracking.

Scripture and mantra content is intentionally not translated. The memorization reader therefore opens with the original Chinese interface and text.
