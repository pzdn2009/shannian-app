Shannian User Guide

Build a clear daily rhythm for chanting, scriptures, meditation and dedication. No account is required, and your practice data stays on your device by default.

1Go deep in one practice; keep the rest flexible

Shannian is not designed to turn practice into an ever-growing checklist. A steadier approach is to keep one or two primary practices as daily anchors, move supporting practices forward over a cycle, and do optional practices when time and energy allow.

Primary

Choose one or two practices you genuinely want to sustain every day. Other items never displace them.

Secondary

Track long scriptures or time-bound practices by cycle total instead of forcing the same amount every day.

Optional

Practice when conditions allow. Optional items do not affect completion and do not create daily pressure.

You can also use Shannian without goals

A practice plan is an optional way to organize your rhythm, not a requirement. Without a plan, you can still open Chant, Scriptures or Meditation from the bottom bar and use all core functions.

The principle is simple: goals serve the practice; the practice does not serve the goals.

2Today's practice

Practice → Practice plan
  1. Set the cycle length and start date.
  2. Choose one or two primary practices and set their daily amounts.
  3. Optionally add one secondary practice and multiple optional practices, then review and start.

Primary completion

Completed items leave the action list. When all primary items are done, a single green summary remains.

Secondary rhythm

Progress is judged against the cycle pace, so the app does not create artificial daily debt.

Optional rotation

Up to three options are shown at once. Refresh to rotate them; completed items stay hidden for the day.

Time estimates

Starting a practice creates a timer from the current target. Once enough history exists, your own average time is used.

Reaching the timed target plays a completion sound. Editing or ending a plan does not delete practice records, scripture progress or personal averages.

3Chant

Bottom bar “Chant” → choose a chant
  • Tap the counter area for +1. After completing a group of ten recitations, switch to “⑩” to record +10.
  • Continuous playback can count automatically. Hundred-count bells and plan completion sounds are configurable.
  • You can add, edit or remove chants. The built-in “南无阿弥陀佛” cannot be removed or duplicated.

Ten-recitation counting

This feature follows the ten-recitation counting method taught by Master Yinguang: count each recitation from one through ten, while reciting clearly, counting clearly and listening clearly. If ten is difficult, use 5+5 or 3+3+4.

“⑩ +10” records a group you have actually completed; it does not replace the recitation itself.

Source: Reply to Layman Gao Shaolin, Letter Four. Read the Chinese source.

4Scriptures

Bottom bar “Scriptures” → book → chapter

Read

Read the segmented text without bookmarks or progress changes caused by scrolling.

Memorize

Move sentence by sentence, play the focused sentence, mark it memorized and keep notes by section.

Recite

Complete chapters, volumes or assemblies in order. Progress changes only when you explicitly mark completion.

Pinyin and pronunciation

Show character-level pinyin from the tools menu and correct individual pronunciations for playback.

Compact cross-platform controls

Chapter estimates use compact labels such as “~15 min”. Memorize and Recite use full-width, low-height icon buttons so the same layout works cleanly on iPhone and Android. The icons keep localized accessibility labels and long-press hints.

Download and edit

  • Open the Online Library to search Deerpark or CBETA and choose chapter-based or original-volume downloads.
  • Downloaded content works offline. Removing local text does not remove saved practice records.
  • Edit Local Scripture works section by section. Please verify the source carefully before saving.

5Meditation

Bottom bar “Meditation” → choose a method
  1. Read the method description and choose 5, 10, 20 or 30 minutes, or a custom duration.
  2. The full-screen timer can pause, resume or end early while saving actual time.
  3. The Six Subtle Dharma Gates open in sequence; you decide when to begin the next stage.
  4. You may add custom methods. Removing a method does not remove its history.

Allow notifications if you want the completion alert while the screen is locked or Shannian is in the background.

6Dedication and records

Dedication

Open it from Practice. The screen summarizes chanting, scriptures, meditation and precepts added since the previous dedication.

Practice records

Open More → Data & Preferences → Practice records to see daily and cumulative data.

Precepts

The lunar-calendar card opens the daily precept confirmation. Precepts are tracked independently from plan completion.

Data integrity

Dedication, renaming, plan adjustments and plan completion never rewrite original practice records.

7Nearby services and events

More → Nearby Vegetarian / Nearby Temples / Events
  • Search vegetarian places and temples by keyword, switch between map and list, focus a selected place, and open system navigation.
  • Location is used only after you start a search. You can still enter an area or keyword if permission is denied.
  • Events support recent, calendar and map views. Confirm details on the temple's official page before attending.

8Settings and data

More → Settings
  • Appearance: system, light or dark theme; Simplified or Traditional Chinese; Chinese or English UI.
  • Reading and playback: memorization entry, scripture text size, speech rate, repeat interval and recitation speed estimate.
  • Chant: playback rate controls continuous audio; counting rate is only the fallback estimate when no personal timing samples exist.
  • Reminders: enable a gentle practice reminder and choose its time.

Backup and restore

More → Data & Preferences → Backup & Restore
  1. Export a backup and save it to a trusted drive or location.
  2. On another device, choose Restore from Document and review the preview before replacing data.
  3. Fully restart Shannian when the restore prompt asks you to do so.

Uninstalling removes local data. Export a backup before changing devices, uninstalling or switching installation channels.
